OpenResearchMetrics

Open science practices of a researcher, documented from OpenAlex, DataCite and the OpenAIRE Graph. Every indicator stands alone, shows how it was computed and what it cannot see. There is no overall score.

Source Indicator catalog (JSON) Schema Cite (DOI)

Requests go straight from your browser to the public APIs. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.

These indicators describe open practices, not research quality. Compare them only with care: disciplines differ, and sources only see what is linked to the ORCID iD.
